ABM Technik + Service offers property-related services. The latest wireless technology enables the monitoring of energy and water consumption. Visual presentation helps you save energy. In addition to the installation and maintenance of water meters and energy meters, the  ABM Technik + Service product portfolio also includes the preparation of heating and operating cost statements, tenant electricity billing, a smoke detector service and drinking water testing.
