ABM Technik + Service offers services relating to buildings. The latest radio technology enables monitoring of energy and water consumption. Visual presentation helps to save energy. In addition to installing and servicing water and energy meters, the ABM Technik + Service product portfolio also covers the preparation of heating and operating cost statements, tenant electricity billing, the smoke detector service and drinking water testing.
