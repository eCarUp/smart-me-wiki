ABM Technik + Service offers services related to real estate. The latest wireless technology enables monitoring of energy and water consumption. Visual presentation helps to save energy. In addition to the installation and maintenance of water and energy meters, the ABM Technik + Service product portfolio also includes the preparation of heating and operating cost statements, tenant electricity billing, smoke detector services and drinking water testing.
