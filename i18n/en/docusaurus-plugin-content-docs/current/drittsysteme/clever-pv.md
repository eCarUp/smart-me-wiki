Clever-PV is an online-based Home Energy Management System (HEMS). Clever-PV allows you to network and intelligently control your electricity generators and consumers via the Internet.

For PV surplus utilisation, it is essential that the current feed-in or draw power from the building's electricity meter is known. The current production output of the PV system is not required for control.

The energy data for control can be streamed quickly and reliably from smart-me meters directly into Clever-PV.

![Clever-PV – figure 1](/img/_en/third-party-systems-clever-pv/01.png)

Target group: Single-family house

Control of:

- Switching contacts

- Charging stations

- Solar inverters

- Heating / air conditioning

- Heat pumps
