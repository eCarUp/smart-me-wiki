## Energy billing with egonline

For buildings, ZEV and charging stations

Egonline is an energy billing platform for everything from small apartment buildings to large industrial sites.

With egonline, you can create electricity cost statements for ZEV (association for own consumption) with smart-me meters and eCarUp charging stations at the push of a button. The same goes for energy cost statements for heating, cold water and domestic hot water, charging stations, the laundry room and general ancillary costs.

You don't want to create the statements yourself? Then we will prepare all energy cost statements for you, determine the solar tariff and check the invoices from the utility.

### Further advantages of egonline:

- Interfaces for exporting the statements to software such as AbaImmo, ImmoTop2, Rimo R5, Microsoft Navision and others

- We support you in configuring the billing. We have the right setting for almost every ZEV, e.g. separate peak tariff and off-peak tariff for solar power, a dedicated tariff for battery power, billing with/without VAT, billing of power peaks, allocation of common-area electricity costs to the users, reporting of solar yield for the system owners, inclusion of advance payment invoices and much more.

- Optional monitoring services ensure that your solar system and heat pump run efficiently and without faults

- Ongoing energy data management: we personally check that all metering data is plausible and complete
