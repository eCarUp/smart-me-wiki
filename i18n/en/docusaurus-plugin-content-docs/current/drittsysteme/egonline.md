## Energy billing with egonline

For buildings, ZEV and charging stations

Egonline is an energy billing platform for small apartment buildings up to large industrial sites.

With egonline you create electricity cost statements for ZEV with smart-me meters and eCarUp charging stations at the push of a button. The same goes for energy cost statements for heat, cold water and domestic hot water, charging stations, the laundry room and the general ancillary costs.

You would rather not create the statements yourself? Then we create all energy cost statements for you, determine the solar tariff and check the invoices from the utility.

### Further advantages of egonline:

- Interfaces for exporting the statements to software such as AbaImmo, ImmoTop2, Rimo R5, Microsoft Navision and others

- We support you in configuring the statements. We have the right setting for almost every ZEV. For example peak and off-peak tariff for solar power separately, a separate tariff for battery power, billing with/without VAT, billing of load peaks, allocation of the general electricity costs to the users, statement of the solar yield for the system owners, inclusion of interim invoices and much more.

- Optional monitoring services ensure that your solar system and heat pump work efficiently and without faults

- Ongoing energy data management: we personally check whether all measurement data is plausible and complete
