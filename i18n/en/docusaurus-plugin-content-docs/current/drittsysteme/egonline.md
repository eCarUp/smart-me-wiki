Energy monitoring & billing with egonline



For buildings, ZEV and charging stations

Egonline is an energy monitoring & billing platform for everything from small apartment buildings to large industrial sites.

You can monitor energy production and consumption and create electricity bills for ZEVs with smart-me meters and eCarUp charging stations in egonline with just one click. Just like energy cost bills for heating, cold and hot water, charging stations, the laundry room and general ancillary costs.

Don't want to create the bills yourself? Then we can prepare all the energy bills for you, determine the solar tariff and check the bills from the electricity company.

Further advantages of egonline:

Interfaces for exporting the bills to software such as AbaImmo, ImmoTop2, Rimo R5, Microsoft Navision and others

We support you with the billing configuration. We have the right setting for almost every ZEV. E.g. separate high and low tariff for solar power, separate tariff for battery power, billing with/without VAT, billing of peak loads, allocation of general electricity costs to users, reporting of solar yield for system owners, inclusion of down payments and much more.

Optional monitoring services ensure that your solar system and heat pump function efficiently and without errors

Ongoing energy data management: we personally check whether all measurement data is plausible and complete
