## Energy billing with egonline

For buildings, ZEV and charging stations

Egonline is an energy billing platform for everything from small apartment buildings to large industrial sites.

In egonline, you create electricity cost billing for ZEV with smart-me meters and eCarUp charging stations at the push of a button. The same applies to energy cost billing for heating, cold water and domestic hot water, charging stations, the laundry room and general ancillary costs.

You don't want to create the billing yourself? Then we create all energy cost billing for you, determine the solar tariff and check the invoices from the utility.

### Further advantages of egonline:

- Interfaces for exporting the billing to software such as AbaImmo, ImmoTop2, Rimo R5, Microsoft Navision and others

- We support you in configuring the billing. We have the right setting for almost every ZEV, e.g. peak and off-peak tariff for solar power separately, a separate tariff for battery power, billing with/without VAT, billing of power peaks, allocation of common area electricity costs to the users, statement of the solar yield for the system owners, inclusion of advance payment invoices and much more.

- Optional monitoring services ensure that your solar system and heat pump work efficiently and without errors

- Ongoing energy data management: we personally check whether all measurement data is plausible and complete
