## Smart charging with the mooveBar

Elmoove is a Swiss company that has been working on e-mobility, e-micromobility and energy management systems since 2017.

The mooveBar is the most innovative product among charging solutions on the e-bike market. Thanks to its modular design, it can be used for certified energy billing in properties. The mooveBar has 4 sockets, which any charger can be plugged into. The sockets are permanently de-energised and must be enabled with RFID cards. This ensures that nobody can charge their vehicle without authorisation. 

For a successful integration, elmoove GmbH can configure the station. 

For a quote, the information you need is available at any time on the elmoove website [www.elmoove.ch](http://www.elmoove.ch).
