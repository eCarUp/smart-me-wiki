Greenpocket GmbH brings together meter data from different systems and enables personalised analyses of the measurement data, e.g. for ISO50001 considerations.

The smart-me measurement data is brought into the Greenpocket software via [automatic MSCONS exports](/schnittstellen/auto-export).

The smart-me MSCONS data contains the following energy sources:

- Electricity 

- Cold water 

- Hot water

- Heat

- Cold


![GreenPocket – figure 1](/img/_en/third-party-systems-greenpocket/01.png)

Functions:

- Data management from multiple sites

- Alarms for exceptional developments at the sites

- Compare data sets according to personalised formulas (e.g. electricity per 100% workplace at sites)

- Create site overviews

- Visualisations

- Automated personalised reports

- Calculate CO2 emissions

- Manual entry of meter data without integration

- User administration
