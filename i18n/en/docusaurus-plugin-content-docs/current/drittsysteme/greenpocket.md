Greenpocket GmbH brings together meter data from various systems and enables personalised analyses of the measurement data, e.g. for ISO50001 assessments.

The smart-me measurement data is transferred to the Greenpocket software via [automatic MSCONS exports](/schnittstellen/auto-export).

The smart-me MSCONS data contains the following energy sources:

- Electricity 

- Cold water 

- Domestic hot water

- Heat

- Cooling


![GreenPocket – Figure 1](/img/drittsysteme-greenpocket/01.png)

Features:

- Data management for multiple sites

- Alarms for unusual developments at the sites

- Comparison of data sets using personalised formulas
    (e.g. electricity per 100% full-time position at sites)

- Creation of site overviews

- Visualizations

- Automated personalised reports

- Calculation of CO2 emissions

- Manual entry of meter data without integration

- User management
