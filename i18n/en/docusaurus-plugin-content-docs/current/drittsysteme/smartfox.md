smart-me devices can be integrated directly and easily into a SMARTFOX installation.

![SMARTFOX – Figure 1](/img/drittsysteme-smartfox/01.png)

By using the intelligent SMARTFOX Pro energy manager, which continuously monitors and controls electricity generation and consumption, the consumption of electrical devices such as heat pumps, charging stations, etc. can be shifted into the sunshine hours, increasing both the self-consumption share and the degree of self-sufficiency.

Target group:

- Single-family house

- Small business

- Multi-party building


Type of connection:

- Direct integration via Modbus TCP for local communication.


Requirement:

- smart-me Professional licenses. [Learn more](/planung/cloud-lizenzen)


![SMARTFOX – Figure 2](/img/drittsysteme-smartfox/02.png)

Functions: 

- Control

- Visualization

- Analysis




Possible applications: 

- High-resolution monitoring of consumers

- High-resolution monitoring of generation systems

- Smart meter function at the house connection point


- Sub-meter for a second control level for dynamic load management of charging stations




## Instructions for linking smart-me meters and SMARTFOX

Complete [linking instructions](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1FaH_zWsVsDhH9CMsMmJ5JkJ0OZN69d2J/view?usp=drive_link) with all the details

### Quick guide

1.  [Find out the IP address of the smart-me meter](/schnittstellen/modbus-tcp#determining-the-ip-address-with-the-dns-name).

2.  Add an external device in SMARTFOX. (select smart-me Telstar)

3.  Enter the IP address of the smart-me meter.

4.  Select the function of the meter.

5.  Save.
