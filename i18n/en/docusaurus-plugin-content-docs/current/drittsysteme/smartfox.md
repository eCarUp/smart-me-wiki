smart-me devices can be directly and easily integrated into a SMARTFOX installation.

![SMARTFOX – figure 1](/img/_en/third-party-systems-smartfox/01.png)

By using the intelligent SMARTFOX Pro energy manager, which continuously monitors and regulates electricity generation and consumption, the consumption of electrical appliances such as heat pumps, charging stations etc. can be shifted to the hours of sunshine and both the proportion of self-consumption and the degree of self-sufficiency can be increased.

Target group:

- Single-family house

- Small business

- Multi-party house


Connection type:

- Direct integration via Modbus TCP for local communication.


Prerequisite:

- smart-me Professional licences. [Learn more](/planung/cloud-lizenzen)


![SMARTFOX – figure 2](/img/_en/third-party-systems-smartfox/02.png)

Functions: 

- Control

- Visualisation

- Evaluation




Possible applications: 

- High-resolution monitoring of consumers

- High-resolution monitoring of generation plants

- Smart meter function at the house connection point

- Sub-meter for second control level for dynamic load management of charging stations




## Instructions for linking smart-me meters and SMARTFOX

Complete [instructions for linking](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1FaH_zWsVsDhH9CMsMmJ5JkJ0OZN69d2J/view?usp=drive_link) the smart-me meters with SMARTFOX.

### Quick instruction

1.  [Find out the IP address of the smart-me meter](/schnittstellen/modbus-tcp#determine-ip-address-with-dns-name).

2.  Add external device in SMARTFOX. (select smart-me Telstar)

3.  Enter the IP address of the smart-me meter.

4.  Select the function of the meter.

5.  Save.
