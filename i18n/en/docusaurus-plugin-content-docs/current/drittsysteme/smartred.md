smartRED is the joint subsidiary of smart-me and ABM. Its focus is on the further development and distribution of smart-me and eCarUp products for the German market.



Since 2017, smartRED and its partner network have successfully implemented over 1,000 tenant electricity systems, enabling the local use of solar power directly on site.



The billing tool also automatically generates legally compliant invoices in Germany. This makes it easy for property managers and landlords to sell locally generated solar power to their tenants – efficiently, transparently and digitally.

Thanks to the usual open interfaces, energy and consumption data can be flexibly integrated into third-party systems such as energy management or billing solutions.

In addition, smartRED has made an important contribution to the expansion of the charging infrastructure with several hundred wallboxes sold and installed in private and (semi-)public spaces.



Today, several thousand charging stations are intelligently managed via the eCarUp backend, ensuring the sustainable and networked operation of electric mobility.

smartRED combines technological innovation with practical experience for a networked, sustainable and decentralised energy future.
