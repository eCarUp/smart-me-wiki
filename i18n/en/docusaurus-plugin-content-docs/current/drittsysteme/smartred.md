smartRED is the joint subsidiary of smart-me and ABM. Its focus is on the further development and sale of smart-me and eCarUp products for the German market.

Since 2017, smartRED and its partner network have successfully implemented more than 1,000 tenant electricity systems, enabling solar power to be used locally, right where it is generated.

With the Billing Tool, legally compliant billing is generated automatically in Germany as well. This makes it easy for property managers and landlords to sell locally generated solar power to their tenants – efficiently, transparently and digitally.

Thanks to the usual open interfaces, energy and consumption data can be flexibly integrated into third-party systems such as energy management or billing solutions.

In addition, with several hundred wall boxes sold and installed in private and (semi-)public spaces, smartRED has made an important contribution to the expansion of charging infrastructure.

Today, several thousand charging stations are managed intelligently via the eCarUp backend, ensuring future-proof and connected operation of e-mobility.

smartRED combines technological innovation with practical experience for a connected, sustainable and decentralised energy future.
