With its product Soleco Optimizer, Soleco AG fulfils its customers wishes regarding self-consumption and energy cost optimisation, supported by smart-me measurement technology.

Applications: Single-family, terraced single-family and apartment buildings.



The advantages:

Reduce energy costs, protect the environment, enjoy comfort. 

Always enough hot water, the house at a pleasant temperature and the electric car charged on time. The Soleco Optimizer takes over these tasks for you in the background, reliably, cost-effectively and with the latest technology.On the user interface, interested parties can find various updated graphic evaluations at any time.The purchase of electricity from the grid is holistically optimised with the weather forecast in terms of electricity tariffs and electricity yield from photovoltaics in interaction with the consumers. This new approach brings significant cost savings, without any loss of comfort.
