The Switzercloud enables the connection and collection of data across different manufacturers, transmission technologies, applications and systems.

Collect data with Switzercloud from IoT devices with a wide variety of transmission paths, from LORAWAN sensors to Ethernet devices and including direct cloud to cloud data transfers (API).



1.  Collect data

2.  Visualise data

3.  Process data

4.  Alert and control


![Switzercloud / Colibird – figure 1](/img/_en/third-party-systems-switzercloud-colibird/01.png)

The Switzercloud can retrieve, collect and process the measurement data from the smart-me portal via API. The processing results in alarms, reports, calculations, controls and/or visualisations with the help of Colibird.
