The TrueEnergy app, together with the smart-me Kamstrup module, allows you to control end devices and car charging stations.

Your car is always charged as much as needed at the appropriate time and charges at the cheapest price, according to available solar power or as fast as possible.

Appropriately, end devices such as lights and washing machines can also be controlled via IFTT.



Target group: Denmark / single-family homes

![TrueEnergy – figure 1](/img/_en/third-party-systems-trueenergy/01.png)

Functions:

- Charging of electric vehicles depending on charging status, times, best prices and available solar power.

- Control of end devices with IFTT according to electricity prices and times.
