Together with the smart-me Kamstrup Module, the TrueEnergy app enables you to control end devices and car charging stations.

Your car always has as much charge as it needs at the right time. It charges at the lowest price, based on available solar power, or as fast as possible.

End devices such as lights and washing machines can also be controlled via IFTT.

Target group: Denmark / single-family homes

</div>
<div className="col col--5 text--center">

![TrueEnergy AS – Figure 1](/img/drittsysteme-trueenergy-as/01.png)

</div>
</div>

Features:

- Charging of electric vehicles based on charge level, times, best prices and available solar power.

- Control of end devices with IFTT based on electricity prices and times
