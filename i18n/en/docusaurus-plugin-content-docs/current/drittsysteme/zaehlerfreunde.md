Zählerfreunde offers tailored solutions for individual installations, tenant electricity projects, energy suppliers and municipal utilities. The solution is based on a web- or app-based platform that allows tenants, landlords and project operators to better monitor and manage electricity consumption and electricity production.

Target group: smart-me users, energy suppliers and tenants with a smart-me account

Note:
Within tenant electricity installations, the tariff switch only works for the tenant electricity installation operator

![Zählerfreunde – Figure 1](/img/drittsysteme-zaehlerfreunde/01.png)

Comprehensive solution

Of course, we support all common browsers. On request, we also publish and maintain your own mobile app: for Android and iOS.

![Zählerfreunde – Figure 2](/img/drittsysteme-zaehlerfreunde/02.png)

Control over consumption

Transparent, cross-utility visualization. Thanks to our modular structure, you can decide which details are shown to your customers and which are not.

- Consumption, costs and instalment payments

- Gas, water and heat

- Tariff management

- Feed-in

- Exchange price

- CO2 level

- CSV export


![Zählerfreunde – Figure 3](/img/drittsysteme-zaehlerfreunde/03.png)

Support for additional devices

Your customers have PV systems, wallboxes, digital electricity meters? We visualize (almost) everything! Users can add further smart devices. Do you offer battery storage, for example? Great – sell it directly via the platform and then have it visualized.

![Zählerfreunde – Figure 4](/img/drittsysteme-zaehlerfreunde/04.png)

Offer new electricity products

Offer your existing customers new tariff products such as flexible tariffs. If required, we calculate whether the customer is still on the best tariff for them. Visualization and tariff management in one place.

![Zählerfreunde – Figure 5](/img/drittsysteme-zaehlerfreunde/05.png)

Simulator

With the Zählerfreunde simulator, customers can test how a specific PV system or battery storage affects their annual costs and their CO2 footprint. The calculations are based on individual consumption and geostationary weather data. If you sell suitable products for this, we will help you offer them via the simulator.

![Zählerfreunde – Figure 6](/img/drittsysteme-zaehlerfreunde/06.png)

Tenant electricity portal solution

Underline your tenant electricity offering with a comprehensive end-customer solution.

- Visualization: All relevant views such as consumption/costs/CO2 etc.

- Add further IoT devices to the dashboard

- Local electricity and residual electricity consumption

- PV share

- Display of consumption bills (+ download option)

- Admin access: Intuitive portfolio overview, CSV export of all metering and cost points, project import and much more


Sustainable tariff switch (Germany only)

Never worry about the tedious tariff switch again!

Switch your electricity tariff either once, or conveniently and automatically every year (certified green electricity guaranteed). This saves you a lot of money every year while supporting a non-profit project of your choice.
