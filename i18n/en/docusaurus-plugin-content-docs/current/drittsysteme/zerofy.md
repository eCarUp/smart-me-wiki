Zerofy is a home energy management system running in the cloud. Zerofy optimizes home energy consumption, solar utilization, and EV charging with smart algorithms. It’s a single app that works across vendors and without any additional hardware.



It integrates with smart-me meters for switching devices on solar-surplus, and for tracking consumption/production. 



Target group: single family homes / smart-me users with account

Operating systems: iOS and Android.

![zerofy – figure 1](/img/_en/third-party-systems-zerofy/01.png)

Key features:

- Integrates with a broad range of solar inverters, EV chargers and EVs, HVAC devices, and smart appliances and smart plugs.

- All that’s needed to connect devices is to log-in to devices with cloud accounts

- Automatically switch devices \*EV charger, heat pump, …) based on excess solar and/or dynamic tariffs with our algorithms

- See top consumers and switch them on/off manually

- Track energy consumption with breakdown by individual connected device

- Track solar production

- Track CO2 emissions overall and per device

- See current grid electricity prices and grid CO2 intensity
