zevvy is online software that helps apartment buildings bill complex energy and ancillary costs easily. Homeowners or their property managers can create bills for solar power (ZEV – association for own consumption), EV charging stations, heating, domestic hot water, cold water and other media in just a few clicks. Further costs for gardening, caretaking, services or common consumption can also be compiled easily and distributed among the residents. Under VEWA, the costs for the zevvy software can be passed on to tenants as ancillary energy costs.
