zevvy helps apartment buildings easily bill complex energy and ancillary costs with online software. Property owners or their property managers can create bills for solar power (ZEV, association for own consumption), EV charging stations, heating, domestic hot water, cold water and other media in just a few clicks. Other costs such as gardening, caretaker, services or general consumption can also be easily listed and allocated to the residents. According to VEWA, the cost of the zevvy software can be passed on to tenants as ancillary energy costs.
