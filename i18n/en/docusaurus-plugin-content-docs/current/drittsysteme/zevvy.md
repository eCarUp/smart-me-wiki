zevvy helps apartment buildings with online software for easy billing of complex energy and ancillary costs. This allows homeowners or their property managers to create bills for solar power (ZEV), EV charging stations, heating, domestic hot water, cold water and other utilities in just a few clicks. Other costs for gardening, caretaking, services or common-area consumption can also be easily listed and distributed among the residents. According to VEWA, the costs for the zevvy software can be passed on to tenants as ancillary energy costs.
