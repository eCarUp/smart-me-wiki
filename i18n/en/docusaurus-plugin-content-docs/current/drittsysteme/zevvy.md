zevvy helps apartment buildings bill complex energy and ancillary costs easily with online software. Building owners or their property managers can create bills for solar power (ZEV), EV charging stations, heating, domestic hot water, cold water and other utilities in just a few clicks. Other costs for gardening, caretaking, services or common-area consumption can also be listed easily and allocated to the residents. According to VEWA, the cost of the zevvy software can be passed on to tenants as energy-related ancillary costs.
