zevvy helps multi-family houses to easily bill complex energy and service charges with online software. With just a few clicks, homeowners or their management can create bills for solar electricity (ZEV), e-charging stations, heating, hot water, cold water and other services. Other costs for gardening, caretaking, services or general purchases can also be easily set up and distributed to the residents. According to VEWA, the costs for the zevvy software can be passed on to the tenants as ancillary energy costs.



zevvy picks up smart-me data directly over API.
