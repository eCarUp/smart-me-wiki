## General settings

Log in to the desired smart-me account and open the settings of the billing section.

Here you configure the general settings for how the invoice document is displayed.
- Currency (always EUR for tenant electricity)
- Tax 
- Your company logo (for display in the invoice document)
- Header / sender
- Footer

### Grid operator

Here you specify who your distribution grid operator is. You should be able to get this information from your electrician/PV system installer or on the internet.
