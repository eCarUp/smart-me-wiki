### Prerequisite

You need a smart-me Professional subscription to create electricity bills under the German Energy Industry Act (EnWG).

This page guides you through billing for **German tenant electricity**. You can find the Swiss variant with ZEV and VEWA under [Billing Switzerland](/konfiguration/billing).

## Configuring billing step by step

Each step has its own page. You can work through them in order, since the bottom of each page tells you what comes next. Or you can jump straight from here to the step you are on.
