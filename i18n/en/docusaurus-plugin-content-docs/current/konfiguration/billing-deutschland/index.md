### Prerequisite

You need a smart-me Professional subscription to create electricity bills in accordance with the German Energy Industry Act (EnWG).

This page guides you through billing for **German tenant electricity**. You can find the Swiss variant with ZEV and VEWA under [Billing Switzerland](/konfiguration/billing).

## Configuring billing step by step

Each step has its own page. You can work through them in order — the bottom of each page tells you what comes next — or jump straight from here to wherever you are right now.
