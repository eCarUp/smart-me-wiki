In the Other (Sonstiges) field, you can add further cost items. This can be done individually for each billing unit (residential unit) or globally for all billing units (property).

Note: Other (Sonstiges) costs are only available with the electricity invoice.

<div className="row">
<div className="col col--7">

## At property level

Example:
A monthly basic fee is to be charged equally to all tenants.

- The entered fee is added to all invoices per month

## At billing unit level

Example:
A charging station is rented out and is to be invoiced monthly to the billing unit.

- The costs are charged only to that one billing unit
