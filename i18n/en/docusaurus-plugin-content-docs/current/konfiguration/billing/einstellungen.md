First, navigate to the billing settings (Einstellungen der Rechnungsstellung).

Here you configure the currency and how VAT is applied.

Specific VAT settings:

- Case A: You are a ZEV (association for own consumption), your electricity sales are definitely below 100'000CHF, and you have not voluntarily registered for VAT:

    \- VAT 0%

    \- Tax already included in prices (Steuer in Preise bereits inbegriffen): YES

- Case B: You are a ZEV and your electricity sales are 100'000 CHF or more, or you have voluntarily registered for VAT:

    \- VAT 8.1%

    \- Tax already included in prices (Steuer in Preise bereits inbegriffen): NO
