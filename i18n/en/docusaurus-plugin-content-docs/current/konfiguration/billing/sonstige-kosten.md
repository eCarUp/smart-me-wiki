In the Other (Sonstiges) field, you can add further cost items. You can do this individually for each billing unit (Abrechnungseinheit) or globally for all billing units (Liegenschaft).

Note: Other (Sonstiges) costs are only available with the electricity bill.

<div className="row">
<div className="col col--7">

## At property level

Example:
80% of the energy supplier's basic cost share is to be charged equally to all participants in connection with the solar tariff.

- The fee entered is added to all invoices per month

## At billing unit level

Example:
A charging station is rented out and is to be invoiced to the billing unit monthly.

- The costs are charged only to that one billing unit
