Additional cost items can be added in the Other (Sonstiges) field. This can be done individually for each billing unit (Abrechnungseinheit) or globally for all billing units (property).

Note: The Other (Sonstiges) costs are only available with the electricity invoice.

<div className="row">
<div className="col col--7">

## At property level
<Menueweg>Billing → Properties & tariffs</Menueweg>

Example:
80% of the energy supplier's basic cost share is to be charged equally to all participants in connection with the solar tariff.

- The entered fee is added to all invoices per month

</div>

## At billing unit level
<Menueweg>Billing → Properties & tariffs → Billing unit</Menueweg>

Example:
A charging station is rented out and is to be invoiced monthly to the billing unit.

- The costs are charged only to that one billing unit
