## 1\. Register the LoRa Gateway on smart-me

1.  Go to [lora.smart-me.com](https://lora.smart-me.com)  and log in with your smart-me account. 

2.  Select “Lora Gateways” and click on “Add”

3.  Enter the name and the device EUI. Alternatively, you can scan the QR code of the EUI with the camera.


![Commissioning LoRa – figure 1](/img/_en/configuration-commissioning-commissioning-lora/01.png)

## 2\. Install and connect the LoRaWAN Gateway to the smart-me LoRa Gateway solution

Internally tested devices:

- [Dragino LPS8N](https://doc.smart-me.com/configuration/commissioning/commissioning-lora/dragino-lps8n)

- [Sensecap M2](https://doc.smart-me.com/configuration/commissioning/commissioning-lora/sensecap-m2)

- [Kerlink Wirnet iFemtoCell-evolution](https://doc.smart-me.com/configuration/commissioning/commissioning-lora/kerlink-wirnet-ifemtocell-evolution)

- [WisGate Edge Lite 2](https://doc.smart-me.com/configuration/commissioning/commissioning-lora/wisgate-edge-lite-2)

- [Milesight UG56](https://doc.smart-me.com/configuration/commissioning/commissioning-lora/milesight-ug56-868mhz)


Note: Untested devices will also work, Gateways just need to offer "Semtech" support and "Data Packet Forwarder" option!

## 3\. Add the meters and sensors

1.   Navigate to the “Lora devices” tab

2.  Select “Add”

3.  Enter the required information for the meter or sensor

4.  Click on “Save”

5.  Check the status of the sensor in the Lora device overview
