## My Energy Map

Create a personalized energy map of your building.

Use an image or a Google Maps view and display selected live values directly in the image.

![My Dashboards – Figure 2](/img/konfiguration-my-dashboards/02_EN.png)

## My Energy Flow

Create a personalized energy flow for your house or for several houses.

Freely choose from all metering points which ones are displayed on each card and get a quick overview of consumption.

![My Dashboards – Figure 3](/img/konfiguration-my-dashboards/03.png)
