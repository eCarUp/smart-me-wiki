## My energy map

Create a personalised energy map of your building.

Use an image or a Google Maps view for this and display selected live values directly in the image.

![My Dashboards – Figure 2](/img/konfiguration-my-dashboards/02_EN.png)

## My energy flow

Create a personalised energy flow for your house or for several houses.

Freely choose from all measuring points which ones are displayed on each map, and get a quick overview of consumption.

![My Dashboards – Figure 3](/img/konfiguration-my-dashboards/03.png)
