## My Energy Card

Create a personalized energy map of your building.

Use an image or a Google Maps view to display selected real-time data directly on the image.

![My Dashboards – figure 2](/img/_en/configuration-my-dashboards/02.png)

## My Energy Flow

Create a personalized energy flow chart for your home or multiple homes.

Freely select which measurement points you want to display on each chart and get a quick overview of your energy consumption.

![My Dashboards – figure 3](/img/_en/configuration-my-dashboards/03.png)
