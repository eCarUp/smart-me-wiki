## Configuring visualizations

A visualization is created on a folder. When you click on the folder in the app or on the web, the corresponding visualization is displayed.

![Visualization and Public Links – Figure 1](/img/konfiguration-visualisierung/01.png)

### Creating visualizations

Proceed as follows to create a visualization:

1.  Log in on the [smart-me website](https://web.smart-me.com/).

2.  Click on Configuration (Konfiguration) at the top right.

3.  Click on Meter / Folder Configuration (Zähler / Ordner Konfiguration).

4.  Select the folder on which the visualization is to be created.

5.  Click on edit (editieren) next to Visualization (Visualisierung).

6.  Select the desired visualization and add the corresponding meters.

### View for tenants

A special view for tenants is available for the virtual tariffs. It shows the current energy consumption (electricity) and its origin (solar or grid). In addition, the bar shows the past electricity source.

This has to be configured additionally for each apartment node.

The visualization is called: "Einfache virtuelle Tarife Wohnung"

Attention: Here you have to select the relevant folder of the apartment, not the meters located within it!

### Simple energy flow

Shows the energy flow for a solar installation, grid consumption and household consumption.

Two of these three meters have to be specified, the third is calculated automatically. If all three meters are specified, incorrect displays may occur. We always recommend specifying direct measurements where possible and not virtual meters.

![Visualization and Public Links – Figure 11](/img/konfiguration-visualisierung/11.png)

### House with several consumers and producers

Up to ten consumers or producers can be visualized for a house.

You can choose to display either the power values (now) or the consumption values of the last 30 or 365 days.

![Visualization and Public Links – Figure 12](/img/konfiguration-visualisierung/12.png)

### Consumption and production monitoring

Shows where the produced energy is consumed (in the house or fed into the grid) as well as where the consumed energy was drawn from (solar or from the grid).

Attention: The main meter must not be a virtual meter!

![Visualization and Public Links – Figure 13](/img/konfiguration-visualisierung/13.jpg)

### Consumption of an apartment compared with a reference apartment

The current power values of an apartment (electricity, heat and water) are displayed.

For the historical consumption values, a comparison with a reference apartment is also displayed (e.g. "10% less electricity consumed than the average").

![Visualization and Public Links – Figure 14](/img/konfiguration-visualisierung/14.jpg)

### Self-consumption rate and self-sufficiency rate

The self-consumption rate and self-sufficiency rate of a building are shown. The values are calculated from the PV meter and the balance meter.

Self-consumption rate: How much of the solar energy is consumed directly in the house.

Self-sufficiency rate: How much of the total consumption is produced by the solar installation.

Attention: The main meter must not be a virtual meter!

## Public Links

With the smart-me Limited or Professional subscription, you can use a Public Link to display the power and/or the meter reading of any measuring point or folder from your smart-me metering infrastructure in real time on a homepage of your choice.

To do so, click on Configuration (Konfiguration) at the top right in the smart-me portal and then select the Public Links tile.

On the interface that now appears, you can use the Add (Hinzufügen) button to determine the name of your Public Link, as well as select the desired measuring point and the display format. The HTML code for embedding it on the homepage of your choice is then created for you. Insert this using copy and paste.
