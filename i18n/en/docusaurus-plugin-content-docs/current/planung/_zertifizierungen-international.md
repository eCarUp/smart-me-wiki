## General product standards and EU regulations

### RED - Radio Equipment Directive and EN 18031

The Radio Equipment Directive must be met by all devices that have interfaces with radio connections and are to be offered in Switzerland or the EU.
It covers, for example, compliance with signal strengths, interference emission and immunity to interference of Wi-Fi, 4G and Bluetooth modules. It also covers the functionality and the security requirements for these modules and products.

The RED (EU) 2022/30 is currently being extended by articles 3.3 d, 3.3 e and 3.3 f. These deal with the aspect of cybersecurity for the network and protection against data theft / data manipulation and hacking.

Since 01.08.2025, the standard EN 18031-1, -2, -3 can be used to meet these supplementary RED articles.

smart-me AG develops all of its products in accordance with EN 18031.

This includes, for example, the following points: 

- Encrypted, authenticated communication from the device all the way to the cloud. 

- Secured and separated areas of the firmware for measurement, communication and display.

- Immutability of the firmware for the metering hardware.

- No unnecessary communication channels that are enabled by default.

- Use of advanced cryptographic chipsets to generate and store cryptographic keys.

- Resilience against external attacks such as DDoS attacks.


### Product standards in general

Product standards generally describe the framework a product must meet with regard to EMC, safety and functionality.
For almost all products there is a defined standard that can be used during development.
However, the standards cannot always be applied precisely to a given product. In some cases, several product standards must be met at the same time.

The product standards used are listed on the product's certificate of conformity.

## Measuring Instruments Directive (MID) - certification

In the conformity assessment procedure, a designated body checks whether the measuring instrument or the manufacture of the measuring instrument meets the legal requirements. The conformity assessment is carried out by the designated body on behalf of the manufacturer of the measuring instrument. The manufacturer then issues the declaration of conformity for the measuring instrument with regard to compliance with a legal standard.

If the measuring instrument is subject to the Measuring Instruments Directive 2004/22/EC (electricity, heat, water, cooling or gas meters), the conformity assessment body must also be notified by the EU Commission.

Such measuring instruments can be identified by the "CE" or "CH" marking in combination with the metrology marking "M".
