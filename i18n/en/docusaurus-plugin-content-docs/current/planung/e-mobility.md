![E-mobility load management – figure 1](/img/_en/planning-e-mobility_1/01.png)

Dynamic load management on the house connection



- The maximum current at the house connection is not exceeded.

- The maximum string currents are statically limited by the charging stations within the same string.

- The house distributon can still be overloaded.

- The connection at the house distributorn should therefore do justice to the maximum currents of both strings together or the maximum consumption of the two strings in the static load management should be throttled on the house distributor.


![E-mobility load management – figure 2](/img/_en/planning-e-mobility_1/02.png)

Dynamic load management on the house distributor



- The maximum current at the house distributon is not exceeded

- The maximum string currents are statically limited by the charging stations within the same string.

- The house connection can still be overloaded

- The house connection must also be dimensioned large in order to withstand the maximum consumption of the charging stations together or reduce the maximum string currents of each string via the static load management to a suitable level.
