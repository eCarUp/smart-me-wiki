Dynamic load management on the house connection



- The maximum current at the house connection is not exceeded. 

- The maximum outgoing-circuit currents are limited statically by the charging stations within the outgoing circuit.

- The house distribution board can still be overloaded.

- The connection at the house distribution board should therefore be dimensioned so that it can withstand the maximum currents of both outgoing circuits together, or the maximum consumption of the two outgoing circuits must be throttled with static load management at the house distribution board.


![E-mobility load management – figure 2](/img/planung-e-mobility/02.png)

Dynamic load management on the house distribution board



- The maximum current at the house distribution board is not exceeded

- The maximum outgoing-circuit currents are limited statically by the charging stations within the outgoing circuit.

- The house connection can still be overloaded in this way

- The house connection must therefore be dimensioned large enough to withstand the maximum consumption of the charging stations together, or the maximum power of each outgoing circuit must be throttled via static load management.
