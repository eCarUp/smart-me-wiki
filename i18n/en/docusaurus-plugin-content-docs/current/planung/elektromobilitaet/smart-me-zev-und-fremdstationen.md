## E-mobility in the smart-me ZEV with third-party charging stations

![smart-me ZEV and third-party stations – Figure 1](/img/planung-elektromobilitaet-smart-me-zev-und-fremdstationen/01.png)

### Description of the load management solution in the metering concept with third-party charging stations

When using third-party hardware, smart-me does not handle the load management.
The manufacturer of the charging hardware provides its own metering solution for control.

Within a smart-me ZEV (association for own consumption), you have to expect that at the points marked with the red arrows, metering hardware for control must be installed in addition to the smart-me meters used to calculate the ZEV.

If, however, the load management is not handled by the respective manufacturer but solved through third-party software, then the smart-me meters can also serve as a reference and information source for the third-party hardware.

### Billing of e-mobility in the ZEV

Billing can be done in various ways, each with its specific advantages and disadvantages:

- Billing in a single-tariff or multi-tariff system in the ZEV with [smart-me Billing](https://web.smart-me.com/energiekostenabrechnung/), either separated from or together with the apartment consumption (depending on the third-party hardware).

- Billing in a single-tariff system using the post-payment method and the [eCarUp backend](https://web.ecarup.com/referenzen/).

- Billing in a single tariff using highly automated credit card billing and the [eCarUp backend](https://web.ecarup.com/referenzen/).
