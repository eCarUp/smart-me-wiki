## E-mobility in the smart-me ZEV with third-party charging stations

![smart-me ZEV and third-party stations – Figure 1](/img/planung-elektromobilitaet-smart-me-zev-und-fremdstationen/01.png)

### Description of the load management solution in the metering concept with third-party charging stations

When third-party hardware is used, smart-me does not take care of the load management.
The manufacturer of the charging hardware offers its own metering solution for control.

Within a smart-me ZEV (association for own consumption), you must expect that, at the points marked by the red arrows, additional meter hardware for control has to be installed alongside the smart-me meters used to calculate the ZEV.

If, however, load management is not handled by the respective manufacturer but by third-party software, the smart-me meters can also serve the third-party hardware as a reference and source of information.

### Billing of e-mobility in the ZEV

Billing in the ZEV (association for own consumption) can be done in different ways, each with its specific advantages and disadvantages:

- Billing in a single-tariff or multi-tariff system in the ZEV with [smart-me Billing](https://web.smart-me.com/energiekostenabrechnung/), separately or together with the apartment consumption (depending on the third-party hardware).

- Billing in a single-tariff system using the post-payment method and the [eCarUp Backend](https://web.ecarup.com/referenzen/).

- Billing at a single tariff using highly automated credit card billing and the [eCarUp Backend](https://web.ecarup.com/referenzen/).
