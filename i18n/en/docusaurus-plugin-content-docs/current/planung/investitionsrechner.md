Use the investment cost calculator to get an idea of your project. The cost calculator uses current market prices and average production data as well as procurement prices for systems from Swisssolar to validate your project.

Based on your input and the prices of our hardware and software, the program calculates roughly when you can expect to reach amortisation and how much you can save through the ZEV (association for own consumption).
