Use the investment cost calculator to get an overview of your project. The cost calculator uses current market prices and average production data as well as system procurement prices from Swisssolar to validate your project.

Based on your inputs and the prices of our hardware and software, the program calculates roughly when you can expect a return on investment and how much you can save through the ZEV (association for own consumption).
