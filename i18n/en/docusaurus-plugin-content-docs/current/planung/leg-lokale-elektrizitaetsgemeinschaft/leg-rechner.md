The decision aid asks in turn for the system size, feed-in,
participants, LEC price and costs, and at the end shows how an LEC (local electricity community) compares
with the current feed-in remuneration. Your entries are not transmitted:
**Save project** (Projekt speichern) stores them as a JSON file on your own computer,
and **Open project** (Projekt öffnen) reads them back in from there.
