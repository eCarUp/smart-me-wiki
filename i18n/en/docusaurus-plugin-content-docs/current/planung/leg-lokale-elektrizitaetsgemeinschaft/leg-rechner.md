The decision aid asks in turn for the system size, feed-in, participants,
LEC price and costs, and at the end shows how an LEC (local electricity community)
compares with today's feed-in remuneration. The inputs are not transmitted:
**Save project** (Projekt speichern) stores them as a JSON file on your own computer,
**Open project** (Projekt öffnen) reads them back in from there.
