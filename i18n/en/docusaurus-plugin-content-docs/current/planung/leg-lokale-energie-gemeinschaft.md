## General information about the LEC

The local electricity community (LEC) is a legal model that enables the exchange and sale of locally produced electricity via the public distribution grid within a regional perimeter.

### The main characteristics:

- Regionality: The electricity exchange is limited to the grid area within a municipality.

- Grid usage: Participants use parts of the distribution grid operator's (DSO) grid. A reduced grid usage tariff applies for this.

- Combinability: An association for own consumption (ZEV) or a virtual ZEV (vZEV) can be integrated into a LEC as a participant.

- Security of supply: LEC consumers remain in the regular basic supply for their residual electricity demand.


### Tariffs and conditions

The pricing for the solar electricity within the LEC is not specified by the DSO, but determined by the community itself. However, the DSO charges a grid usage fee for the energy transmitted. The maximum discount on the grid usage fee is currently 40%. (As of Q1 2026)

You can find detailed information as well as the VSE industry recommendation on LEC at: [www.lokalerstrom.ch](http://www.lokalerstrom.ch)
