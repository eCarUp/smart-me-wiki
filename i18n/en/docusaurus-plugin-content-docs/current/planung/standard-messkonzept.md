## Tariffing and design of the standard metering concept

The metering concept is tariffed via the balancing meter and production, or alternatively via consumption / PV or battery with reference to the balancing meter.

The metering concept is complete as soon as 100% of all consumption and production is measured. The balancing meter at the building's grid connection serves as a correction and as a reference.

### Validation and billing by the energy supplier

The total grid electricity sold roughly corresponds to the amount of electricity billed by the energy supplier.

The amount of electricity fed back in the export register of the balancing meter roughly corresponds to the amount in the feed-in remuneration of the energy supplier.
