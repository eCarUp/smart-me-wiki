## Tariff calculation for the standard metering concept

The tariffs for the metering concept are calculated using the balance meter and production. Alternatively, they can be calculated using consumption / PV or battery, with the balance meter as a reference.

### Tariff calculation with solar tariff incl. vZEV

The balance meter is referenced for the tariff calculation.

If you use the solar tariff incl. vZEV, you do not need any additional licences for virtual meters.

Balance meter (Bilanzzähler)

The house connection meter is referenced as the balance meter.

![Standard metering concept – Figure 2](/img/planung-standard-messkonzept/02.png)

Production meter (Produktionszähler)

All production meters and battery meters are recorded as production.

![Standard metering concept – Figure 3](/img/planung-standard-messkonzept/03.png)

### Tariff calculation using the solar tariff (consumption / PV) with or without battery tariff (consumption / battery)

The balance meter is ignored for the tariff calculation. It is only used later for verification.

If you use the solar tariff (consumption / PV), you need additional licences for virtual meters.

At a minimum, you need the total consumption. You create it as a virtual meter made up of all participating outgoing circuits of the tenant electricity setup.

Total consumption = Apartment 1.1 + Apartment 1.2 + Apartment 2.1 + General + Parking space 1 + Parking space 2 + Heat pump

Under Production (Produktion), add the solar system meter, or the battery meter if you use the battery tariff.

Under Total consumption (Gesamtverbrauch), enter the virtual total consumption meter you created.

The balance meter is deliberately referenced here to increase accuracy!

![Standard metering concept – Figure 4](/img/planung-standard-messkonzept/04.png)

### Validation and billing by the energy supplier

The total grid electricity sold roughly matches the amount of electricity billed by the energy supplier.

The amount of electricity fed back in, recorded in the export register of the balance meter, roughly matches the amount in the energy supplier's feed-in remuneration.
