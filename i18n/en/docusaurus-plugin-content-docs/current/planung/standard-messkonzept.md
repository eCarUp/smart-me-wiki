## Tariffing of the standard metering concept

The metering concept is tariffed using the balancing meter and production, or alternatively using consumption / PV or battery with reference to the balancing meter.

### Tariffing with solar tariff incl. vZEV

The balancing meter is referenced for the tariff calculation.

When using the solar tariff incl. vZEV (Solartarif inkl. vZEV), no additional licences for virtual meters are required.

Balancing meter

The building connection meter is referenced as the balancing meter (Bilanzzähler).

![Standard metering concept – Figure 2](/img/planung-standard-messkonzept/02.png)

Production meter

All production meters and battery meters are recorded as production (Produktion).

![Standard metering concept – Figure 3](/img/planung-standard-messkonzept/03.png)

### Tariffing using solar tariff (consumption / PV) with or without battery tariff (consumption / battery)

The balancing meter is ignored for the tariff calculation; it is only considered later for verification.

When using the solar tariff (consumption / PV), additional licences for virtual meters are required.

At a minimum, the total consumption is required. It is created as a virtual meter consisting of all participating tenant electricity outgoing circuits.

Total consumption = Apartment 1.1 + Apartment 1.2 + Apartment 2.1 + General + Parking space 1 + Parking space 2 + Heat pump

Under Production (Produktion), the solar system meter is added, or the battery meter in the case of the battery tariff.

Under Total consumption (Gesamtverbrauch), the virtually created total consumption is entered.

The balancing meter is deliberately referenced here to increase accuracy!

![Standard metering concept – Figure 4](/img/planung-standard-messkonzept/04.png)

### Validation and billing of the energy supplier

The totals of the grid electricity sold roughly correspond to the amount of electricity billed by the energy supplier.

The amount of electricity fed back in the export register of the balancing meter roughly corresponds to the amount in the feed-in remuneration of the energy supplier.
