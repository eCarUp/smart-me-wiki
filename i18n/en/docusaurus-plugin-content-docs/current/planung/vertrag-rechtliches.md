## Contractual basis for a ZEV (association for own consumption) in Switzerland

The ZEV (association for own consumption) is a contractual association of several end consumers in order to consume the electricity they produce themselves on site.

In an association for own consumption, the end consumers share a single grid connection. Towards the distribution grid operator, all end consumers participating in the respective ZEV appear as a single customer.
