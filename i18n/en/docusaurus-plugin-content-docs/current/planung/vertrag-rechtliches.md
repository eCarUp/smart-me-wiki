## Contractual basis for a ZEV (Switzerland)

The ZEV (association for self-consumption) is a contractual association of several end consumers to use the electricity they produce themselves on site. 

In the case of an association for self-consumption, the end consumers have a common grid connection. All the end consumers participating in the respective ZEV act as a single customer towards the electricity grid operator.
