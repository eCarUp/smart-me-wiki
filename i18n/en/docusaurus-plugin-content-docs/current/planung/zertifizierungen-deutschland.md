## Certifications for charging stations in Germany

Charge points in rental properties:

For charging station hardware used in a rental property, Germany generally has the same requirements as for standard meter hardware:

- MID certification is required before the recorded energy can be billed.




Charge points in public areas with different users:

- In Germany, public charge points must comply with calibration law (Eichrecht). Calibration law compliance requires that the meter hardware in the charging station has MID certification.

## Calibration law (Eichrecht)

Calibration law (Eichrecht) is a German law that governs the accuracy and transparency of measurements in Germany. For electric vehicle charging, this regulation ensures that measurements at charging stations are accurate and transparent in order to protect consumers. 

Under calibration law, EV charging stations must have an electricity meter that is certified and calibrated (MID).
The electricity meter measures the energy consumed and determines the amount charged to the EV driver. 

Calibration law also governs other aspects of billing. Charging stations must display prices clearly and give customers a receipt that shows the amount of energy consumed, the price charged and other relevant details.

It must be possible to verify the correctness of charging sessions using [independent transparency software](/informationssicherheit/zählertransaktionen).

Calibration law also addresses data security and energy losses during charging sessions caused by cabling or other factors.

## Tenant electricity

Billing tenant electricity has the same meter hardware requirements as described above: an MID-certified measuring device is required. To find out how to set up and bill tenant electricity in smart-me, see [Tenant electricity](/konfiguration/billing/mieterstrom).
