## Certification for charging stations in Germany

Charge points in rental relationships:

For charging station hardware used in a rental relationship, Germany generally requires the same as for standard meter hardware:

- MID certification, so that the recorded energy may be billed.




Charge points in public areas with different users:

- In Germany, public charge points must comply with the Eichrecht. The basis for Eichrecht compliance is that the meter hardware in the charging station has MID certification.

## Eichrecht

The Eichrecht is a German law that regulates the accuracy and transparency of measurements in Germany. When charging electric vehicles, this regulation ensures that the measurements at the charging stations are accurate and transparent in order to protect consumers. 

Under the Eichrecht, EV charging stations must be equipped with an electricity meter that is certified and calibrated (MID).
The electricity meter measures the energy consumed and determines the amount charged to the EV driver. 

The Eichrecht regulations also govern other aspects of billing. Charging stations must clearly display prices and provide customers with a receipt stating the amount of energy consumed, the price charged and other relevant details.

It must be possible to verify the correctness of the charging sessions using [independent transparency software](/informationssicherheit/zaehlertransaktionen).

The Eichrecht also covers data security and energy losses during charging sessions caused by cabling or other means.

## Tenant electricity

For billing tenant electricity, the same requirements for meter hardware apply as above: an MID-certified measuring device is the basis. How to set up and bill tenant electricity in smart-me is described under [Energy Billing (Germany)](/konfiguration/billing-deutschland).
