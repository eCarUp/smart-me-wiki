## Meter certifications in Switzerland

This section summarises the regulations on legal metrology and MID that apply in Switzerland. You can find the ordinance here: [FDJP Ordinance on Measuring Instruments for Electrical Energy and Power (EMmV)](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2015/578/de)

### Load profile certification

The CH load profile certification (CH-Lastgang) allows the user to bill on the basis of meter readings taken at intervals of at least 15 minutes. This certification requires additional technical capabilities and covers the signing, data storage and transmission paths (security, encryption) of the data.

[3-Phase Meter Telstar](/produkte/telstar-80a), [3-Phase Meter Telstar CT](/produkte/telstar-ct) and [Pico EV Charger](/produkte/pico-ladestation) hold both the MID and the CH load profile certification.

## Certifications in a ZEV (association for own consumption)

smart-me technology covers both of the following variants:

### Variant 1: ZEV billing using meter readings and a single or dual tariff (MID required)

A ZEV can be billed using the meter reading at the start and the meter reading at the end (cut-off dates). For this, the law only requires a measuring device with MID certification. Tariff differences with a peak and off-peak tariff are only possible with an external signal; otherwise a single tariff can be used.

Advantages:

- The measuring device only needs to be MID certified. ([smart-me 3-Phase Meter Telstar)](/produkte/telstar-80a)

- Simple billing of the meter readings on cut-off dates.

- A simple software solution is possible, or it can even be done manually.


Disadvantages of a single tariff:

- Costs can only be charged according to the quantities consumed.

- Tenants cannot optimise their costs by using electricity carefully at the right time.

- No targeted use of solar power by the tenants. (Lower efficiency in internal electricity sales)


Disadvantages of a dual tariff with an external tariff signal:

- Costs can only be allocated according to the quantities consumed in the HT and NT tariffs using an external control signal. (No solar tariff)

- Tenants have only limited options to optimise their costs by using electricity carefully at the right time.

- No targeted use of solar power by the tenants. (Lower efficiency in internal electricity sales)




### Variant 2: ZEV billing using load profiles and a multi-tariff system (MID and CH load profile certification required)

A ZEV can be billed using load profile data (15-minute intervals). This variant unlocks the full scope of a ZEV with all its benefits for tenants and ZEV owners.

Advantages:

- Allows billing with different tariffs. (NT, HT, solar tariff)

- Allocates the costs incurred to the party that caused them. (Also takes into account the availability of solar power with a special tariff)

- Tenants can optimise their costs themselves and focus on solar power.

- The self-produced solar power can be sold internally more effectively, improving the ROI.

- Differentiated electricity tariffs make the property more attractive to tenants overall.


Disadvantages:

- Requires MID and load profile certified measuring devices. ([smart-me 3-Phase Meter Telstar)](/produkte/telstar-80a)

- Requires capable software to store, evaluate and process the load profile data.

## Certifications for charging stations in Switzerland

Charge points in a tenancy:

For charging station hardware used in a tenancy, Switzerland generally requires the same as for standard meter hardware:

- MID certification to bill the recorded energy at a single tariff.

- CH load profile certification + MID certification to bill the recorded energy with multiple tariffs (HT, NT, solar tariff).




Charge points in public areas with different users:

- In Germany, the legal situation has been defined and requires conformity with calibration law (Eichrecht). (see [Certifications Germany](/planung/zertifizierungen-deutschland)).
    In Switzerland, there are currently no legal requirements regarding MID conformity.

## Re-verification of MID meters

All information is subject to change and in accordance with [EMmV 941.251](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2015/578/de)  (as of 1 January 2018).

### Meters

Meters must be re-verified by the Federal Institute of Metrology (METAS) or by an authorised verification body as follows, using the procedure set out in Annex 7 Section 1 MessMV: 

a. Meters with an electronic measuring element: every 10 years ([smart-me 3-Phase Meter Telstar)](/produkte/telstar-80a)

b. Meters with an electromechanical measuring element: every 15 years 

### Transformers (current sensors)

Transformers must be re-verified by METAS or by an authorised verification body as follows, using the procedure set out in Annex 7 Section 1 MessMV: 

a. Inductive transformers with a non-split core: every 60 years

b. Transformers other than those under letter a: every 2 years. 



### Extending the validity of an existing verification

The certification is valid for 10 years, regardless of the manufacturer. 

Lot-based verification (statistical testing procedure)

SAK and CKW re-verify smart-me meters using the lot-based procedure for a fee. If you are interested, please contact the heads of the respective verification bodies directly. In practice, most meters in a ZEV are not re-verified but are depreciated over the years and replaced with new meters at the end of their service life.

Lot-based testing requires an initial sample check after 5 years and then periodically every 5 years.

If METAS approves the lot in accordance with letter E Section 6, the meters of the approved lot are deemed verified for a further 5 years, provided they remain subject to the statistical testing procedure. 

### Consequence

If the verification of the devices expires and a lot-based verification procedure is not possible, the meters must be replaced with new devices after 10 years.

## MINERGIE certification with smart-me

MINERGIE certification requires a suitable metering system for the required monitoring. 

smart-me is a certified module according to Minergie and can be used directly to fulfil the monitoring requirements.

The smart-me system also has the automated interface for transferring data to the Minergie database for the Minergie Monitoring + extension.
