## Meter certifications in Switzerland

This section summarises the rules on legal metrology and MID that apply in Switzerland. You can find the ordinance here: [FDJP Ordinance on Measuring Instruments for Electrical Energy and Power (EMmV)](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2015/578/de)

### Load profile certification

The CH load profile certification (CH-Lastgang) lets the user bill on the basis of meter readings taken at intervals of no more than 15 minutes. This certification requires additional technical capabilities. It covers the signing and storage of the data and the transmission paths (security, encryption).

The [3-Phase Meter Telstar](/produkte/telstar), the [3-Phase Meter Telstar CT](/produkte/Telstar-CT) and the [Pico EV Charger](/produkte/pico-ladestation) hold both the MID certification and the CH load profile certification.

## Certifications in an association for own consumption (ZEV)

smart-me technology covers both of the following variants:

### Variant 1: ZEV billing with meter readings and a single or dual tariff (MID required)

A ZEV can be billed using the meter readings at the start and end of a period (reference dates). For this, the law only requires a measuring device with MID certification. Different tariffs are only possible as a peak and off-peak tariff with an external signal. Otherwise a single tariff can be used.

Advantages:

- The measuring device only needs MID certification. ([smart-me 3-Phase Meter Telstar)](/produkte/telstar)
- Simple billing of the meter readings on reference dates.
- A simple software solution is possible, or billing can be done manually.


Disadvantages of a single tariff:

- Costs can only be allocated by consumption volume.
- Tenants cannot lower their costs by using electricity carefully at the right time.
- Tenants cannot make targeted use of solar power. (Lower efficiency in selling electricity internally)


Disadvantages of a dual tariff with an external tariff signal:

- Costs can only be allocated by consumption volume in the HT and NT tariffs, using an external control signal. (No solar tariff)
- Tenants can only lower their costs to a limited extent by using electricity carefully at the right time.
- Tenants cannot make targeted use of solar power. (Lower efficiency in selling electricity internally)




### Variant 2: ZEV billing with load profiles and a multi-tariff system (MID and CH load profile certification required)

A ZEV can be billed using load profile data (15-minute intervals). This variant gives you the full scope of a ZEV, with all its benefits for tenants and ZEV owners.

Advantages:

- Allows billing with different tariffs. (NT, HT, solar tariff)
- Allocates the costs to whoever caused them. (Also takes the availability of solar power at a special tariff into account)
- Tenants can lower their own costs and focus on using solar power.
- Self-generated solar power can be sold internally more effectively, which improves the ROI.
- Differentiated electricity tariffs make the property more attractive to tenants overall.


Disadvantages:

- Requires MID-certified and load-profile-certified measuring devices. ([smart-me 3-Phase Meter Telstar)](/produkte/telstar)
- Requires capable software to store, evaluate and process the load profile data.

## Certifications for charging stations in Switzerland

Charge points in rental properties:

When charging station hardware is used in a rental property, Switzerland generally has the same requirements as for standard meter hardware:

- MID certification to bill the recorded energy at a single tariff.
- CH load profile certification + MID certification to bill the recorded energy at multiple tariffs (HT, NT, solar tariff).

Charge points in public areas with different users:

- In Germany, the legal situation has been defined and requires compliance with calibration law (Eichrecht). (see [Certification Germany](/planung/zertifizierungen-deutschland)).
    In Switzerland, there are currently no legal requirements for MID compliance.

## Re-verification of MID meters

All information is subject to change and is based on [EMmV 941.251](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2015/578/de)  (as of 1 January 2018).

### Meters

Meters must be re-verified by the Federal Institute of Metrology (METAS) or by an authorised verification office, using the procedure in Annex 7 Section 1 MessMV, as follows: 

a. Meters with an electronic measuring element: every 10 years ([smart-me 3-Phase Meter Telstar)](/produkte/telstar)
b. Meters with an electromechanical measuring element: every 15 years 

### Transformers (current sensors)

Transformers must be re-verified by METAS or by an authorised verification office, using the procedure in Annex 7 Section 1 MessMV, as follows: 

a. Inductive transformers with a non-split core: every 60 years
b. Transformers other than those under letter a: every 2 years. 

### Extending the validity of an existing verification

The certification is valid for 10 years, regardless of the manufacturer. 

Verification by lot (statistical testing procedure)

SAK and CKW re-verify smart-me meters by lot for a fee. If you are interested, please contact the heads of the verification offices directly. In practice, most meters in a ZEV are not re-verified. Instead, they are depreciated over the years and replaced with new meters at the end of their service life.

Testing by lot requires an initial sample test after 5 years, and then another one every 5 years.

If METAS approves the lot under letter E section 6, the meters in the approved lot are considered verified for another 5 years, provided they remain subject to the statistical testing procedure. 

### Consequence

If the verification of the devices expires and a lot verification procedure is not possible, the meters must be replaced with new devices after 10 years.

## MINERGIE certification with smart-me

MINERGIE certification requires a suitable metering system for the required monitoring. 

smart-me is a certified module under Minergie and can be used directly to meet the monitoring requirements.

The smart-me system also has the automated interface for transferring data to the Minergie database for the Minergie Monitoring + extension.
