The smart-me 1-phase meter 80A is an energy meter with integrated WiFi interface for the transmission of real-time data. The meter synchronizes the measured values automatically and encrypted into the smart-me cloud. The smart-me portal can be used to further process the data. Alternatively, they can be exported and processed in third-party systems via our open interface. The meter has a digital output for controlling devices with a dry contact.

![Single Phase Meter 80A – figure 1](/img/_en/products-single-phase-meter/01.jpg)

## Functions

- [Commissioning](/konfiguration/inbetriebnahme) with the free smart-me app.

- Control with [if/then actions](/konfiguration/wenndann-aktionen) or [event actions](/konfiguration/wenndann-aktionen/ereignisaktionen)

- [Visualizations](https://doc.smart-me.com/configuration/visualisations)

- [Dry contact outputs](/schnittstellen/ein_und_ausgaenge) for control of external devices, one of them with 8A relay

- Floating contact input for tariff signal or [digital input](/schnittstellen/ein_und_ausgaenge)

- [Interfaces](/) via API, CSV, MSCONS and IS-E

- Encrypted real-time data connection to the smart-me cloud

## Display

The counter alternately displays different values.

- "So XXXX" is the installed software version.

- "CH XXXX" is the software checksum.

- "Number + T1/T2" is the respective tariff value.

- "Er XX" is an error code.

## Configuring inputs and outputs

The Single Phase Meter 80A has one digital output and one digital input, which can be used as pulse inputs and outputs or as a switchable dry contact. Details can be found on the [Inputs and Outputs](/schnittstellen/ein_und_ausgaenge) wiki page.
