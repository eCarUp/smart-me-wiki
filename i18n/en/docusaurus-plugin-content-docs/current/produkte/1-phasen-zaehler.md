The smart-me Single Phase Meter 80A is an energy meter with an integrated WiFi interface for transmitting real-time data. The meter synchronizes the measured values automatically and encrypted to the smart-me Cloud. The data can be exported to third-party systems and processed further in the smart-me Portal or via our open interface. The meter has a digital output for controlling potential-free devices.

![Single Phase Meter 80A – figure 1](/img/produkte-1-phasen-zaehler/01.jpg)

## Functions

- [Installation](/konfiguration/inbetriebnahme) with the free smart-me App.

- Control with [if/then actions](/konfiguration/wenndann-aktionen) or [event actions](/konfiguration/wenndann-aktionen/ereignisaktionen)

- [Visualizations](/konfiguration/visualisierung)

- [Potential-free contact output](/schnittstellen/ein_und_ausgaenge) for controlling external devices

- Potential-free contact input for tariff signal or [digital input](/schnittstellen/ein_und_ausgaenge)

- [Interfaces](/) via API, CSV, MSCONS and IS-E

- Encrypted real-time data connection to the smart-me Cloud

## Display

The meter alternately shows different values.

- "So XXXX" is the installed software version.

- "CH XXXX" is the checksum of the software.

- "Number + T1/T2" is the respective tariff value

- "Er XX" is an error code.

## Configuring inputs and outputs

The Single Phase Meter 80A has one digital output and one digital input, which can be used as pulse inputs and outputs or as a switchable potential-free contact. You will find details on the wiki page [Input and Output Configuration](/schnittstellen/ein_und_ausgaenge)

## FAQ

### At what interval do the meters send data?

- With Basic or Limited licenses: max. 1x per minute.

- With Pro licensing: max. 1x per second


### Can I reset the meter reading to zero?

No, since our meters are used for billing, it is not possible to reset them.
