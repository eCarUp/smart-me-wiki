The Landis+Gyr module brings the measurement data of the L+G E450 and E350 energy meters into the smart-me Cloud.

No additional hardware is required for the module. It uses the existing WiFi network and connects directly to the smart-me Cloud. This is the easiest way to analyse and optimise energy consumption in real time on your smartphone, tablet or computer.

With a Professional subscription, the meter values can also be requested via the Modbus TCP interface.

![Landis+Gyr Module – figure 1](/img/_en/products-landis-gyr-module/01.jpg)

## Functions

- Real time visualisation of power, accumulated meter reading (purchase and supply), tariffs 1 and 2 reactive power and reactive energy

- Automatic storage of meter readings in the cloud

- Storage and automatic synchronisation of measurement data in case of connection failures to the cloud

- Encrypted Wi-Fi connection directly to the smart-me cloud

- Contact output to control external devices

- The smart-me cloud enables extensive energy data management, automatic invoicing, remote controlling and alarms

- Easy installation using the free smart-me app available for Android and iOS

## Technical Data

Supported meters: L+G E450 and L+G E350 Interfaces with E35C module

Operating voltage: provided by the energy meter

Interface: WiFi

Data storage: 2 months

Firmware update: Available online

Product certification: CE

Wi-Fi standard: 802.11 b/g/n

Wi-Fi security standard: WEP, WPA, WPA2 (personal)

Size: 84 × 40 × 23 mm

Weight: 37 g

Temperature range: from −40 to +70 °C

Installation: On or next to the L+G meter

## Successor product

smart-me does not offer a successor product for L&G meters. 

If you operate a smart-me ZEV and require a new solution for your metering (e.g. HAK), the L&G module must be replaced by a [Telstar 80A](/produkte/telstar) or [Telstar CT](/produkte/Telstar-CT).

If measurements are carried out in the private sector for monitoring, home automation, etc., the [Telstar 80A](/produkte/telstar) or [Telstar CT](/produkte/Telstar-CT) can be installed at any time. If you are looking for a solution with your existing L&G meter, we recommend you take a look at this page. [https://gplug.ch/](https://gplug.ch/)
