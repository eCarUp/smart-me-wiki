Sales discontinued on 30.04.2021, support and cloud support are guaranteed until 30.03.2025.

The Landis+Gyr Module brings the measurement data of the L+G E450 and E350 energy meters into the smart-me Cloud.

No additional hardware is required for the module. It uses the existing WiFi network and connects directly to the smart-me Cloud. This is the easiest way to analyse and optimise energy consumption in real time on a smartphone, tablet or computer.

With a Professional subscription, the meter values can also be queried via the Modbus TCP interface.

![Landis+Gyr Module – Figure 1](/img/produkte-landis-gyr-modul/01.png)

## Functions

- Real-time visualization of power, total meter reading (consumption and delivery), tariff 1 and 2, reactive power and reactive energy

- Automatic saving of the meter readings in the cloud

- Storage and automatic synchronization of the measured values if the connection fails

- Contact output for controlling external devices

- Encrypted WiFi connection directly to the smart-me Cloud

- Comprehensive energy data management, automatic billing as well as control and alarms via the smart-me Cloud

- Simple installation with the free smart-me App for Android and iOS

## Technical Data

Supported meters L+G E450 and L+G E350 with E35C module

Upload interval 5 seconds

Operating voltage supplied by the meter

Interfaces WiFi

Data storage 2 months

Firmware update possible online

Product certification CE

WiFi standard 802.11 b/g/n

WiFi security standard WEP, WPA, WPA2 (Personal)

Dimensions 84 × 40 × 23 mm

Weight 37 g

Temperature range −40°C to 70°C

Mounting Next to or on the L+G meter


Note: A 9V battery (9-volt block) is required briefly for the installation.

## Configuring the CII interface

The CII interface (customer interface) of the Landis+Gyr meter (e.g. E450 or E350) must be activated. Follow the instructions to configure the interface correctly.

## Changing the upload interval

- Log in to the smart-me portal

- Select the meter

- Select the gear icon at the top right

- General settings (Allgemeine Einstellungen)

## Successor product

smart-me does not offer a successor product for L&G meters.

If you operate a smart-me ZEV (association for own consumption) and need a new solution for your metering (e.g. main service box), the L&G module must be replaced by a [Telstar 80A](/produkte/telstar) or [Telstar CT](/produkte/Telstar-CT).

If measurements are taken in the private sector for monitoring, home automation and so on, the [Telstar 80A](/produkte/telstar) or [Telstar CT](/produkte/Telstar-CT) can be installed at any time. If you are looking for a solution with your existing L&G meter, we recommend taking a look at this page. [https://gplug.ch/](https://gplug.ch/)
