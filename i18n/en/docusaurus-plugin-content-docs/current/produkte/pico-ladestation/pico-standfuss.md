## Order information

Each Pico stand consists of one stand:

212070-PL or 232070-PL 

and a head for mounting the Pico (all heads are compatible with both stand variants)

- 212070-PL/PH1

- 212070-PL/PH2

- 232070-PL/PH1

- 232070-PL/PH2


## Stand variants

### 212070-PL Pico Stand Basic

Universal stand with base plate according to OPI-2020 standard

- Mounting of 1-2 Pico charging stations.

- Supply lines directly from fuse box to charging station.


Mounted on a concrete base with holes as per instructions. Details under Downloads Pico.

Surface: hot-dip galvanised, powder-coated and painted in RAL 9004 signal black

![Pico Stand – figure 2](/img/_en/products-pico-ev-charger-pico-stand/02.png)

### 232070-PL Pico Stand with maintenance door

- Universal stand with base plate to OPI-2020 standard

- Service cover for easy multiple wiring and inspection

- Space for terminal blocks and PE terminals

- Space for 2 residual current circuit breakers, RCD/LCB combination devices, lightning breakers type-2 or other DIN devices.

- Inlet and outlet: max. 25mm2

- IP44

- IK10

- Cable strain relief

- comes with matching terminal blocks and PE terminals


Mounted on a concrete base with holes as per instructions. Details under Downloads Pico.

Surface: hot-dip galvanised, powder-coated and painted in RAL 9004 signal black

## Head variants

### 232070-PL/PH1 and 232070-PL/PH2 Pico heads with integrated cable dock

In addition to the cable hook for rolling up the cable, this head variant has plug holders to store the loose end of the cable in a water-protected place after the charging process.

This head is particularly suitable for public charging stations with a fixed cable at the charging station.

The heads fit all stand variants.

![Pico Stand – figure 5](/img/_en/products-pico-ev-charger-pico-stand/05.jpg)
