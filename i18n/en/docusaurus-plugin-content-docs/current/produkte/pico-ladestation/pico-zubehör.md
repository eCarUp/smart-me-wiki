## 212070/VP Pico preparation package

The Pico preparation package allows you to pre-install additional charging stations for later use. The scope of delivery includes the base plate and a blank cover.

As soon as an expansion is due, the Pico electronics without mounting plate can be reordered under article number 212070/1.
To mount the Pico electronics, click them into the mounting plate and screw them to it.
