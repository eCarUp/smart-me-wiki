### Requirement

You need one eCarUp Premium subscription per station to bill the imported data or transfer it to other systems.

You can find a detailed overview on the eCarUp wiki page [15-minute data import](https://ecarupwiki.smart-me.com/funktionen/15-minuten-datenimport).
